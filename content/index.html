About us

A consulting practice built for public infrastructure

Urban Engineering (Pty) Ltd was founded in early 2018 to deliver consulting services in civil, structural and transportation engineering. Our primary focus is professional services to local, provincial and national government entities, as well as private clients in the Southern Cape and Karoo regions.

More about the practice

Our vision is to offer cost-effective solutions in civil and structural engineering, fostering job creation and enhancing South Africa's infrastructure network.
